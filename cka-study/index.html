From August 8, 2026, I am shifting the active focus of the technical residency toward preparation for the Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) exam.

This study track is intended to deepen hands-on Kubernetes administration, Linux, container orchestration, troubleshooting, cluster operations, and cloud-native platform engineering.

This page will be expanded as the study progresses.

Current Focus

Primary study platform:

  • KodeKloud CKA curriculum

Primary technical areas will include:

  • Kubernetes architecture;
  • cluster installation and configuration;
  • workloads and scheduling;
  • services and networking;
  • storage;
  • security contexts and access control;
  • cluster maintenance;
  • troubleshooting;
  • command-line fluency with kubectl;
  • Linux and container-runtime fundamentals that support Kubernetes administration.

Study Approach

The emphasis will be on practical administration rather than exam memorization.

learn concept
  -> perform lab
  -> troubleshoot failures
  -> repeat without guidance
  -> document useful patterns

Where useful, I will connect the study back to systems already present in the technical portfolio, particularly MockCo and future cloud-native deployment work.
